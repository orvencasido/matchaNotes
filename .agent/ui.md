## Aesthetic Direction: Matcha & Off-White
- **Color Discipline**:
  - Background: Warm ceramic off-white (`#FBFBF9`).
  - Elevated surfaces / side panels: Soft oatmeal/linen cream (`#F4F3EE`).
  - Accent / Primary elements: Ceremonial matcha green (`#2D4739` and `#4E6E58`).
  - Badges & Active States: Soft matcha wash (`#E8EFE8`) with deep matcha text (`#23392D`).
  - Typography Color: Deep spruce ink (`#19221C`) for primary, muted slate (`#5C6861`) for secondary.
  - Borders: Crisp, ultra-thin hairlines (`#E4E3DC`).
- **Typography**:
  - High-clarity modern typography with strict line heights and generous tracking.
  - Zero unnecessary headers, no repetitive subtitles, no noisy "Welcome back" or "Start writing your journey here" filler copy.
- **Interactions**:
  - Tactile, crisp hover states and smooth active presses.
  - Clean 6-digit PIN pad with subtle keypress animations.
  - Clean image gallery with lightbox preview and subtle rounded borders.
